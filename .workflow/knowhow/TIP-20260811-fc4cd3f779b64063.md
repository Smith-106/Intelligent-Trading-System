When feeding discrete TPSL barrier param spaces (tuples of candidate values, including single-point axes) into validation_gate/Optuna, do not use optimize_method=bayesian. Optuna expects continuous (low, high) ranges and raises IndexError on len(spec)<2 or mis-parses multi-value lists. Use optimize_method=grid, or skip in-gate optimize for a fixed barrier and evaluate fixed entries/exits. Also ensure StrategyOptimizer._grid_values handles len(spec)==1.
